01 — Information

What we collect

We collect the information you provide to create and use an account: your name, email address, phone number if you add one, profile details, account role, and communications with other members. We also store the businesses, listings, images, video, saved opportunities, follows, and messages you create.

Like most websites, we receive technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as device and browser details, IP-derived security information, and activity needed to diagnose errors or prevent abuse.

02 — Purpose

How we use it

We use account information to authenticate you, operate the marketplace, show your profile and listings, enable messaging, provide support, maintain security, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information or use your sign-in information to serve targeted advertising.

We keep information only for as long as needed for these purposes, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. When you delete your account, we delete the account and the associated marketplace records described below, except where retention is necessary for those limited purposes.

03 — Social sign-in

Google and Apple sign-in

Google sign-in is an optional way to create or access a GoDyrect account; email/password sign-in remains available. Apple sign-in may be added later. We request only the basic identity information required to sign you in—typically a provider account identifier, verified email address, and, where shared, your name and profile image. We never receive or store your Google or Apple password.

We will use that information solely to create, authenticate, and secure your GoDyrect account, and to prefill your profile if you choose. We will not use Google sign-in data for advertising, sell it, or request additional Google data unless a future feature clearly explains the request and asks for the relevant permission. Our use of Google data will be limited to the practices described in this notice.

With Sign in with Apple, you may choose Apple’s private relay email address. That address can forward mail while keeping your personal address private; we will treat it as your account email and will not ask you to reveal your personal email simply to use the service.

04 — Visibility

Sharing & public content

Your public profile details, business pages, listings, and listing media can be visible to marketplace visitors. Do not include confidential financial, personal, or deal information in public listings. Messages are shared with the participants in that conversation.

We use service providers to host the application and data, including Cloudflare for delivery and Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. We may disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users, or the public.

05 — Your controls

Your choices and deletion

You can update your profile and account role in Settings. You can remove public content you created through its edit controls. To permanently delete your account and associated marketplace data, open Settings → Leave GoDyrect, confirm the deletion, and submit the request. This removes your profile, listings, businesses you created, saved opportunities, follows, messages, and uploaded marketplace media. Account deletion cannot be undone.

For access, correction, or questions about personal information that the in-product controls do not cover, email support@godyrect.com from the address associated with your account.

06 — Marketplace terms

Use GoDyrect responsibly

GoDyrect is a marketplace and communication platform, not a broker, lender, escrow service, or party to a transaction. Members are responsible for their listings, communications, due diligence, compliance, and transactions. Do not post unlawful, misleading, infringing, or confidential material you do not have permission to share. We may remove content or restrict accounts to protect the marketplace or enforce these terms.

We may update this notice as the product changes. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will update this page and provide additional notice where appropriate.
